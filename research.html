```
Research

Understanding galaxy evolution

My research combines observations, spectral energy distribution modelling and radiative transfer to investigate the physical properties of galaxies.

```

```
PhD project

SE3D

SE3D focuses on panchromatic resolved observations of galaxies using three-dimensional models of stars and dust.

Traditional SED modelling generally treats a galaxy as an unresolved source. SE3D instead aims to retain spatial information, allowing galaxy properties to vary across the galaxy.

The project combines observations across multiple wavelengths with three-dimensional modelling and radiative transfer to investigate stellar populations, dust attenuation and galaxy structure.
```

```
Research areas

Key interests

Galaxy evolution

How galaxies form and evolve across cosmic time, and how their stellar and dust components are connected.

Stellar populations

Spectral energy distribution modelling and the inference of stellar masses and other galaxy properties.

Dust attenuation

Understanding the role of dust geometry, attenuation and radiative transfer in interpreting galaxy observations.

JWST & HST

Panchromatic imaging and spatially resolved analysis using observations from HST and JWST.

Radiative transfer

Using three-dimensional radiative transfer to connect physical galaxy models with observable quantities.

Computational astrophysics

Developing Python-based analysis and modelling workflows for large astronomical datasets.
```
