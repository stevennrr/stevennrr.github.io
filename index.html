Research interests

What I work on

✦

Galaxy Evolution

Investigating how galaxies form, grow and evolve across cosmic time, with particular interest in the relationship between stars and dust.

◉

HST & JWST

Using panchromatic observations from HST and JWST to study galaxies across multiple wavelengths and resolve their internal structure.

⌬

3D Modelling

Developing three-dimensional models of stars and dust to connect spatially resolved observations with the physical properties of galaxies.

PhD research

SE3D

SE3D explores panchromatic resolved galaxy observations using three-dimensional models of stars and dust.

The project aims to move beyond treating galaxies as single unresolved sources by incorporating their spatial structure into spectral energy distribution modelling.
